Full Steam Ahead! is a tiny humorous game about a pompous steamboat captain who demands ever more speed. You are the unseen stoker shovelling coal to make the boat go faster and faster.

Slow down and the captain gets angrier until he fires you. Your score is your top speed in knots.

This game was developed for Ludum Dare 60 (theme: Old Tech).

## How to play

- Press **Space** or **Enter** to shovel coal and make the boat go faster!
- Hold **Up** arrow or **W** to go up, release to fall.
- Keep shovelling, or the captain fires you.
- Don't crash!
